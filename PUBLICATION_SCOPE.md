# 公开范围 / Publication scope

[中文说明](README.md) · [English overview](README.en.md)

本仓库发布 Obsidian 学习库 `00`–`04` 内的学习笔记、论文卡片、术语与教学内容。为尊重原作者权益，未上传论文 PDF、整篇原文转写、整篇翻译/中英对照、论文整页或图表截图、原始提取文件、元数据导出与缓存。也未上传 Zotero 数据库、应用配置、附件目录和管理目录。

本地笔记中关于“已归档全文”“完整读本”的描述仅说明本地资料状态，不表示这些材料包含在公开仓库中。指向未发布内容的链接在公开副本中指向本说明；请根据论文卡片里的 DOI、arXiv 或出版商链接访问原始论文。此筛选不构成对全部内容版权或学术准确性的认证。

公开副本已将双向链接转换为 GitHub 可点击链接，并移除本机绝对路径。原始本地笔记保持不变。论文的观点、术语与结果仍归原作者；学习笔记中的解释与例题应与论文原文及实际实验结果区分。

This repository contains learning notes, paper cards, terminology and teaching material from folders `00`–`04`. It excludes paper PDFs, complete transcriptions, full translations/bilingual reproductions, paper page/figure screenshots, extraction outputs, metadata exports, caches, Zotero databases and app settings. References to full readers describe the local collection only. Links to excluded local material lead here; use the original DOI, arXiv or publisher links instead. This selection is not a copyright or academic-accuracy certification.

Wikilinks have been converted to GitHub links and machine-specific absolute paths removed in this public copy. Local originals are unchanged. Paper claims and results remain attributable to their authors; teaching explanations and exercises are study material, not independently reproduced experimental findings.
