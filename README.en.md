## Reading the notes

Browse the Markdown files on GitHub, or clone the repository and open the folder in Obsidian. YAML properties and mathematical notation are retained. Wikilinks in this public export are converted to standard Markdown links for portability.

## Scope and attribution

This export keeps study summaries, terminology, teaching notes and worked examples with references to original sources. It excludes complete paper transcriptions/translations, paper screenshots and extraction outputs. Some local figures and full readers are outside the public scope; their links lead to the scope notice. The notes are primarily in Chinese; the language switch applies to the README, not a complete translation of all notes.

These are evolving study records, not claims of authorship over cited research or evidence that model training and experimental reproduction have been completed. Third-party rights and source attributions remain with their respective owners; no blanket license is granted over third-party material.
